A Letter For You
On Your Special Day

There's something a lot of people miss about—specifically, your attitude.

Most people glance at the fact that you're naive, too soft, all of that—and they try to take advantage. From a general perspective, most people would do the same. But when I got to notice that, I felt attracted to it. Like a necessity, not a want.

As time went on, that need grew. The feeling grew. It turned to love—or something I couldn't understand. In a sense, it wasn't me, but it went on. Over time, it changed my view on love. Often, because of how I felt, I'd think: "I need to change my mind and thought process, maybe my actions"—considering how stubborn I am. But sometimes I did.

There's a quote that explains the feeling:

"For one human to love another: that is perhaps the most difficult of all our tasks, the ultimate, the last test and proof, the work for which all other work is but preparation. Love does not at first mean merging, surrendering, and uniting with another (for what would a union be of two people who are unclarified, unfinished, and still incoherent?), it is a high inducement for the individual to ripen, to become something in himself, to become world, to become world in himself for the sake of another person."

I love you, baby. I can't describe it enough, but I wholeheartedly do.

I wish you a wonderful birthday today and forever more.

I know you were disappointed by what happened with your service and overwhelmed by everything. I'm sorry I couldn't be beside you. But I'm proud of you for handling it like the queen you are.

Happy 17th birthday—a big leap and even bigger jumps to come.

I always remember: sometimes when things are out of place for me, just a conversation with you makes everything better. "You complete me." I couldn't have said it better.

I love you without knowing how, or when, or from where.
I love you directly, without problems or pride:
I love you like this because I don't know any other way to love,
except in this form in which I am not and you are not,
so close that your hand upon my chest is my hand,
so close that your eyes close as I fall asleep.

And I hope it continues to burn with passion across all our birthdays and ages.

Forever yours,
Divine
